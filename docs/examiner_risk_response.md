## Genuine limitations retained

No ablation establishes classifier necessity. The data/corpus are synthetic or authored, with shared authorship and a small policy-aware personal subset. Semantic top-1 gains are modest and the engineering objective was missed. The 15-case model review is gold-conditioned, author-reviewed and potentially anchored; the strict intended rubric does not mechanically reproduce every accepted human PASS. Guardrail development used known cases and still has an out-of-domain failure. No productivity study, selection-friction measurement, large-corpus evaluation, live monitoring or production-generalization evidence exists. Local hashes are not external preregistration. Comprehensive redaction and calibrated confidence rejection were not delivered.

These limitations remain in the submission. They are not reasons to tune frozen tests or replace original labels. The [future pilot](evaluation.md#not-yet-evaluated-end-to-end) is a proposal only. No commit or push accompanies this documentation response.
