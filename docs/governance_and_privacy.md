# Governance and privacy

This is an academic prototype, not a production service or a compliance certification. Public/synthetic Bitext data and generalized tickets avoid publishing or sending real support records for this experiment. **Synthetic data does not prove compliance or production safety.**

- **Data boundary:** use fictional/generalized inputs and authored DemoRetail policies. Confidential employer documents, internal metrics and identifiable customer transcripts are prohibited. Raw work/course documents and raw API logs are excluded from this public copy.
- **Minimization:** ask only for decisive safe facts and masked references. Do not request passwords, OTPs, full payment-card details or bank credentials. Limited pattern checks do not provide comprehensive PII detection/redaction.
- **Secrets:** `.env` and Streamlit secrets are ignored; `.env.example` has only a placeholder. The offline app does not read a key or make inference calls. No key is needed to run replay. Never log authorization headers or publish credentials.
- **Authority:** verified policy and the human reviewer are control points. The classifier, retriever and LLM are not policy authorities. Every draft requires review; no customer message, refund, cancellation, account change or queue submission is performed.
- **Policies:** cards record ID, version, fictional owner, effective date, review status and review due date. The app checks selected evidence against its trusted hash and current date. Missing, altered, unapproved or stale evidence blocks drafting. Change policy through a reviewed new version, not silent edits to frozen evidence.
- **Evaluation:** frozen benchmarks, unchanged outputs and final human adjudication remain separate. Abin approved Stage 8 labels and Stage 9 conclusions. Failures are preserved, including the novel-domain guardrail fixture failure. No post-score tuning is presented as untouched evaluation.
- **Guardrails:** abstain, clarify, escalate, human_review and multi_policy_review are distinct outcomes. Business escalation is separate from mandatory review. Unknown facts and contradictions must not be guessed into a resolution. DR-HUM-002 is procedural, not a default policy answer.
- **Auditability:** public evidence includes final per-layer metrics, all 15 unedited component outputs with human labels, behavior results and rule version/hash. Transport/authentication logs remain private. Publication-derived views are labeled and source hashes retained.
- **Prompt injection:** customer wording and extracted text are untrusted data. Prompts and finite rules resist recognized overrides; no adversarial robustness claim is made. No agent tools exist.
- **Retention/access:** this demo relies on the owner's laptop file access, not deployed multi-user access control. Proposed local retention review is after assessment/appeal needs end; no deletion automation is claimed. New tickets stay in app session memory and are not deliberately written to disk. Public Git history is persistent, so sanitize before committing.
- **Rollback:** a future correction requires a new version and separate regression evidence. Preserve old artifacts. If a new version fails, hold drafts for humans rather than bypassing checks.
- **Vendor dependency:** the completed FM experiment used OpenRouter with OpenAI-only routing. The alias, prices, availability and processing terms may change. No zero-retention guarantee is assumed. Any future live mode needs explicit approval and reverified governance; none exists here.

Remaining gaps include finite vocabulary/negation coverage, incomplete privacy and OOD detection, unsupported prerequisites and temporal anchors that need human interpretation. The UI scope warning is a separate heuristic, not a new scored guardrail version. No real-traffic, productivity, privacy-compliance or production-readiness claim is supported.
